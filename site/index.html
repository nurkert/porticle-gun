PorticleGun

Two portals. One gun. Every wall is a door.

A Minecraft server plugin that adds a portal gun, an interdimensional gun and a gravity gun, each with its own 3D model for Java and Bedrock players.

Download See it in action

Spigot or Paper 1.21.4+ · Java 21 · Bedrock through Geyser · free and open source

Portals

Point, click, step through

Left-click places your first portal, right-click the second, on any wall, floor or ceiling. Walk, fall or fly through: speed and view direction carry over, so you can fling yourself across the map. Mobs, items and arrows travel too.

Every gun owns its pair of portals. Two rings around the crosshair show which ones are open.

Pick your colours

A gun in your colours

The model is built block by block, and its lights follow your portals: claw tips in the primary colour, windows and emitter in the secondary one, the moment you change them in the gun's menu.

Gravity gun

Pick it up. Carry it anywhere.

Press the drop key with the gun in hand and lift animals, monsters or whole blocks. Chests keep their contents and spawners their mob, even across restarts. Drop the gun itself with a quick double tap.

Interdimensional gun

Tear a rift into the air

The big brother of the PorticleGun opens wobbling green rifts: one in front of you, one at the destination. Friends can follow before they close. Sneak and use the gun to choose where to go.

  • Any world a random, safe landing spot
  • World spawn the spawn point of any world
  • Homes up to seven, set with one click
  • Back where you last stepped in
  • Last death straight back to your things

Portal fluid

Fuelled by the End

Distil three rift shards into portal fluid and pour it into the tank from the gun's menu. The glass dome on top shows how much is left. In the inventory every item shows an animated pixel icon:

Rift atlas

Rift straight to a stronghold

Insert a rift compass and the gun is attuned for ten minutes: rifts cost nothing, and the next one opens right next to the nearest structure you pick. Underground ones are entered through a safe cave.

Controls

Java and Bedrock, side by side

Bedrock players join through Geyser and Floodgate and get the same guns, the same 3D models and controls made for controllers and touch screens. Menus only ever need a single click or tap.

Download

Up and running in a minute

  1. Download the plugin jar below.
  2. Drop it into your server's plugins folder and restart.
  3. Run /porticlegun and take your gun, or craft one.
Download PorticleGun

Release files

Java players get the resource pack on join. With Geyser on the same server the Bedrock pack is installed automatically, so the extra files are only needed for self-hosting.
